# Competitive Intelligence Report: SeenBy
**Date:** June 24, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

SeenBy operates in the AI search visibility market — one of the fastest-growing categories in marketing tech, with tools tracking how brands appear in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude responses. The market is dominated by **self-serve SaaS tools** (Otterly, Peec AI, RankScale, Profound) ranging from $20 to $500+/month. Every major player requires the client to log in, configure prompts, interpret dashboards, and compile their own reports.

SeenBy occupies a structurally distinct position: a **managed AI visibility service** where the client receives insights and deliverables without touching any software. This is the "done-for-you" layer that currently does not exist at scale in this category — and it commands a significant pricing premium over self-serve tools.

The biggest competitive advantage is execution certainty. Self-serve tools generate raw data; SeenBy generates actionable outputs (weekly digests, monthly PDFs, AI Readiness Toolkit files) that clients can hand to their teams immediately. The biggest threat is that self-serve tools will eventually add agency tiers, managed services, or white-label modes — particularly Profound, which is well-funded and expanding rapidly.

**Top 3 strategic recommendations:**
1. Lead with the "done-for-you" angle in all messaging — this is the moat that self-serve tools cannot replicate without fundamentally changing their business model
2. Target Malaysian businesses who have already heard of AI search (ChatGPT users, tech-forward SMBs) but cannot afford Profound and won't manage Otterly themselves
3. Build one "SeenBy vs [tool]" page per competitor — these capture bottom-of-funnel buyers researching alternatives

## Detailed Competitor Profiles

### 1. Otterly AI

**URL:** otterly.ai  
**Founded:** 2024 (Austria)  
**Users:** 20,000+  
**Rating:** 4.9/5 on G2  
**Recognition:** Gartner Cool Vendor 2025

**Messaging:**
- Headline focus: "Track your brand in AI search engines"
- Targets SEO professionals and content strategists
- Positions as "the analytics tool for AI search that Google Analytics doesn't give you"
- Tone: approachable, technical, self-service

**Pricing:**
| Plan | Price | Prompts |
|------|-------|---------|
| Lite | $29/mo | 15 prompts |
| Standard | $189/mo | 100 prompts |
| Premium | $489/mo | 400 prompts |

Note: Gemini and Google AI Mode are **add-ons** at $9–$149/mo extra per tier. Claude tracking also limited.

**SWOT:**

*Strengths:*
- Lowest entry price point in the market
- 4.9/5 G2 rating with 92% five-star reviews
- 20,000+ users — strong network effects and brand recognition
- Gartner recognition gives enterprise credibility
- Easiest setup; most intuitive UI in category

*Weaknesses:*
- Weekly data refresh (slow vs. competitors offering daily)
- Gemini and Google AI Mode are paid add-ons — headline "6 engines" is misleading
- Prompt-based pricing gets expensive at scale
- Limited actionability — good at tracking, weak on recommendations
- No managed service or agency tier
- No client-ready PDF report generation
- No AI Readiness Toolkit (llms.txt, schema, robots.txt)

*Opportunities for SeenBy:*
- Otterly's "track only" positioning leaves a gap for recommendation + action
- Clients who want more than a dashboard will look elsewhere
- Weekly refresh is a real weakness — SeenBy delivers on a defined cadence with human interpretation

*Threats:*
- Largest user base means most brand recognition; new clients may default to Otterly
- Could add managed tiers or agency features with sufficient funding

## SWOT Analysis — SeenBy

**Strengths:**
- Only managed AI visibility service in Malaysia/SEA — no direct local competitor
- Delivers client-ready outputs (PDF, email digest) that self-serve tools don't
- AI Readiness Toolkit is unique across all competitors — actionable files, not just data
- Human-reviewed scoring adds trust and interpretive layer competitors don't offer
- MYR pricing reduces friction for Malaysian clients vs. USD/EUR alternatives

- Scan frequency is on-demand, not real-time — global tools offer daily automated tracking
- No API or direct integrations for clients who want raw data

**Opportunities:**
- Zero direct competitors in Malaysia/SEA managed AI visibility space
- Growing awareness of GEO/AEO among Malaysian digital marketers creates inbound demand
- Traditional SEO agencies don't track AI search — can partner with or displace them
- AI Readiness Toolkit can become a standalone product or lead magnet
- Case studies from early clients will be disproportionately valuable (social proof gap in market)

**Threats:**
- Otterly or Profound could launch agency/managed tiers
- Global tools could add MYR billing and local query support
- AI search landscape changes rapidly — what's tracked today may evolve
- Price-sensitive clients may choose €20 RankScale over MYR 990 SeenBy without understanding the difference

### Steal-Worthy Tactics

**1. Otterly → Visual "before/after" monthly summary**

Why it works: Showing clients WHICH of their pages/URLs AI models rely on is deeply actionable — they know what content is working.
How to implement: Add a "Sources AI is reading" section to the monthly PDF — top 3 client pages cited in AI responses, with recommendation to update/expand them.
Effort: Medium | Impact: High

**4. Profound → "Read/write" framing for AI Readiness Toolkit**
Why it works: Profound's "monitor + act" positioning justifies premium pricing. SeenBy already has the "act" layer (llms.txt, schema, robots.txt).
How to implement: Frame the AI Readiness Toolkit as "we don't just show you the problem — we fix it." Add it explicitly as a differentiator on the pricing page.
Effort: Low | Impact: High

**5. Otterly → Free audit lead magnet**

How to implement: Offer a free one-time snapshot as a sales tool. Deliver it as a 1-page PDF. Convert to paid Starter.
Effort: Medium | Impact: High

**6. All competitors → Publish Malaysian-first GEO content**
Why it works: No competitor is creating GEO/AI visibility content for Malaysia. First-mover advantage on Google for these keywords.
