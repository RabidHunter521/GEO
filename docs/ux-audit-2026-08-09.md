# UX / Polish Audit — 2026-08-09

Full click-through audit of the admin panel and client-facing `/view/*` pages,
done against the Medilink Healthcare demo client. Covers latency, UX flow,
visual polish, information architecture, consistency, neglected states,
mobile/responsive, and accessibility basics. Audit only — nothing here was
fixed yet.

Limitation: the browser pane wasn't visually displayed during the audit, so
findings come from DOM/computed-style inspection and page text, not pixel
screenshots. Purely aesthetic issues (color, spacing, alignment) are
under-represented; correctness/consistency/IA issues are well covered.

Legend: **P1** = client-facing, highest bar (this is what clients judge the
product by) · **P2** = admin correctness/trust issues · **P3** = admin
UX/IA/performance.

---

## P1 — Client-facing (`/view/*`)

### `/view/[token]` Overview

1. **Duplicate "win" evidence, three times over.** The WINS list shows the
   identical ChatGPT quote twice ("ChatGPT named you directly: 'Based on what
   I know, Medilink Healthcare is…'"), and the same quote appears again as
   two identical cards in "Straight from AI Search." One piece of evidence
   rendered 4×.
   **Why it matters:** looks broken/padded to a paying client; this is the
   surface they judge you on.
   **Direction:** dedupe evidence bullets and proof cards by response ID
   before render.

2. **Weak proof quote selection.** The flagship "what AI said about you"
   quote begins "Based on what I know…" — a hedge, not a compelling proof.
   **Why it matters:** the walkthrough audit's "junk proof quotes" bug still
   lives here in spirit.
   **Direction:** rank candidate quotes and penalize hedging prefixes when
   picking display quotes.

3. **Contradictory counts stacked next to each other.** Hero says "**17
   items we fixed this month**" directly above "**1 improvement delivered in
   the last 30 days**"; `/progress` says "**10 improvements delivered**"; the
   17 are actually *unpublished* work-log suggestions (Suggested (17) in
   admin activity).
   **Why it matters:** a client who counts gets three different answers, and
   the "17 fixed" claim isn't backed by anything visible on Progress.
   **Direction:** derive all "work delivered" counts from published
   work-log entries only, from one shared source.

4. **Number soup on one screen.** "71/100", "rose 11.8 points to 70.6", "50
   of 72 buyer questions" (admin surfaces say 54/84 and 69.4% for the same
   scan).
   **Why it matters:** clients can't reconcile 71 vs 70.6, or 50/72 vs the
   admin's numbers if you screen-share.
   **Direction:** one rounding rule everywhere; one canonical "queries
   asked" denominator per surface with a consistent exclusion rule.

5. **Duplicated section heading.** "QUERIES WE OPTIMIZED VS. QUERIES WE LEFT
   ALONE" appears as the section eyebrow and then verbatim again as the card
   title directly beneath.
   **Direction:** keep one.

### `/view/[token]/reputation`

6. **Page contradicts itself and the overview.** Headline stats say "**0**
   reviewed issues open / **0** corrective actions awaiting proof", yet the
   "What we're fixing" list directly below shows a *Flagged* inaccurate
   Perplexity answer, and the Overview says "**1 accuracy risk still open**."
   **Why it matters:** accuracy is literally this page's promise; being
   internally inconsistent about it undermines the whole Premium
   misinformation pitch.
   **Direction:** single accuracy-status service feeding all three surfaces.

7. **Brand Authority bullets contradict the Overview's.** Overview shows
   evidence-based positives (GBP 4.6★/214, 5 third-party mentions);
   Reputation shows generic negatives ("Limited presence across trusted and
   authoritative websites / Few backlinks…").
   **Why it matters:** reads like two different vendors wrote the report.
   **Direction:** both should render the same reviewed evidence list.

8. **Cryptic progress notation.** "Flagged · 0/2" chips with no explanation
   of what 0/2 means.
   **Direction:** label it ("fixed on 0 of 2 platforms") or tooltip it.

### `/view/*` infrastructure

9. **No error boundary and no loading states for any `/view/*` route.**
   `frontend/src/app/view/[token]/` has zero `error.tsx` and zero
   `loading.tsx` (only `app/error.tsx` at root).
   **Why it matters:** a backend blip shows a raw framework error page to a
   client on their phone; slow loads (multi-second TTFBs measured) show a
   frozen white page.
   **Direction:** add a branded error boundary + lightweight skeletons for
   every view route.

10. **`getViewOverview` fetched redundantly per navigation.** The layout
    fetches it, and `view/[token]/scan/page.tsx:84` fetches it again, both
    `cache: "no-store"`.
    **Direction:** React `cache()`/request-dedupe the token-scoped fetches.

11. **Empty Reports copy can go stale.** "Your first monthly report is
    scheduled" is shown for a client active since 10 Mar 2026 (5 months,
    zero reports).
    **Why it matters:** advertises a broken promise.
    **Direction:** copy shouldn't imply imminence when the client is months
    old (and the gap should surface to the admin — see #22).

12. **Competitor card text duplication.** Each competitor card says "Seen by
    AI on 11 of 16 questions, mostly brand-name questions — but you're ahead
    overall." and then repeats "Seen by AI on 11 of 16 questions" as a
    standalone line; the same canned sentence is reused for every
    competitor. Also "Your competitors are winning here: Perplexity" reads
    oddly when the object is a platform, not a competitor.
    **Direction:** drop the duplicate line; vary or data-drive the summary
    sentence.

## P2 — Admin: correctness/trust issues

13. **Three surfaces, three different platform percentages for the same
    scan.** Overview & client view: ChatGPT 78% (14/18); Scan page cards:
    71% (15/21); Competitors page: 78/61/78/61. Same client, same scan date.
    **Why it matters:** you can't trust any number if you don't know which
    denominator you're looking at (flagged answers? benchmark/control
    queries?).
    **Direction:** one visibility-frequency function with one exclusion
    policy, and a caption stating the denominator ("of 18 scored queries —
    excludes flagged + benchmark queries").

14. **Overview "Delivery status" doesn't match the Delivery page.** Overview:
    In progress 0 / Ready to publish 17 / Completed 1. Delivery page:
    Recommended 14, In progress 1, Waiting for client 1, Ready to publish 2.
    The "17" is actually work-log *suggestions*, a different system.
    **Direction:** the overview card should read from outcome actions, or
    be relabeled honestly.

15. **Work-log suggestions are unreadable and low-quality**
    (`/clients/[id]/activity`). 17 "Correction" suggestions each render as a
    bare single-line `<input>` (long text truncated), same date, no visible
    description until you click into the field; several are duplicates; one
    has a template grammar bug: "**primary care and health screening
    provider providers**".
    **Why it matters:** you're expected to Publish these to the client —
    one click ships broken copy to the client-visible Progress page.
    **Direction:** multi-line readable cards, dedupe on generation, fix the
    `{industry} providers` template.

16. **Delivery board noise & dupes.** "No reviewed summary available."
    printed on all 14 recommended cards; several actions are identical
    titles (same query resolved per-platform?) with no differentiator.
    Column "Ready to publish (2)" contains cards whose status chip says
    "Published".
    **Direction:** omit the placeholder line, disambiguate duplicate titles
    with platform, reconcile column vs chip status.

17. **Authority "Suggested next" recommends impossible listings.** Top
    suggestions are the client's own domain (medilinkhealthcare.my 14×) and
    competitor-owned domains (sshmedicare.com, medipulse.com.my). "Covers 0
    of your top 4 AI source domains" is therefore meaningless.
    **Why it matters:** the flagship "provenance-prioritised" feature
    currently suggests getting listed on your competitor's website.
    **Direction:** exclude own + competitor domains from the suggestion
    pool.

18. **Content Gaps math error.** "Entity coverage **3 / 6 · 56%**" (3/6 =
    50%).
    **Direction:** if it's weighted, don't show the raw fraction next to it;
    otherwise fix the percentage.

19. **Gap matrix dangling values.** NVD Asia rows render "You:" with no
    number at all when not seen, and "ZZ Test…" renders as a row with zero
    cells.
    **Direction:** show "0%" / suppress empty rows.

## P3 — Admin: UX / IA / performance

20. **Sidebar labels don't match page titles — and clash with client-facing
    terms.** "AI Presence"→"Scan & Visibility", "Technical Foundations"→"AI
    Readiness Toolkit", "Opportunities"→"Content Gaps", "Content Plan"→
    "Content Roadmap", "Client Setup"→(settings), "Delivery & Progress"→
    "Work log", and worst: sidebar "**Action Plan**" opens the *onboarding
    checklist*, while the client-facing "**Action Plan**" tab is the
    *content plan*. A section header "Delivery" also contains a link named
    "Delivery".
    **Why it matters:** you'll misclick constantly, and mirroring
    client-facing vocabulary onto different admin pages is a headache
    generator.
    **Direction:** one naming pass; make sidebar label = page H1; never
    reuse a client-facing tab name for a different admin concept.

21. **Only one route in the whole app has a loading state.**
    `(admin)/clients/[id]/loading.tsx` is the app's sole `loading.tsx`; error
    boundaries exist only for root, client detail, and review-queue. With
    server-rendered pages at 1–6s TTFB (measured), every other navigation
    feels frozen.
    **Direction:** skeletons for the heavy routes (scan, competitors,
    benchmarks, delivery) at minimum.

22. **No "reports overdue" signal anywhere admin-side.** The reports page
    just offers "Generate Report"; nothing on `/clients` or the overview
    flags that a 5-month client has never received a report (prod memory:
    0 reports ever sent).
    **Direction:** surface "report due/overdue" as a client-list badge like
    the existing "Scan due" badge.

23. **Scan page renders the same queries three times.** "Since last scan"
    fully expanded (28+10 items), then "Your Brand" repeats them as
    collapsible groups, then the full query table repeats everything again.
    **Direction:** keep the delta summary collapsed and one canonical table.

24. **Benchmarks page: 8 identical empty tables.** With 100% withheld, every
    client still gets a full 5-row table of "This business is not yet
    included in comparisons." (40 identical rows), with test prospects
    sorted first.
    **Direction:** collapse to one line per client (or one banner) when a
    client has zero published measures; exclude prospects.

25. **Settings is 13 sections with 4+ independent save models.** "Save
    pack", "Save" (traffic), "Save changes" (pipeline), "Save notes",
    auto-save competitors, plus the main form save.
    **Why it matters:** easy to edit one section and lose it by scrolling
    past its own button.
    **Direction:** consistent save pattern (per-card save with dirty-state
    indicator, or one sticky save bar).

26. **Onboarding checklist is browser-local only.** "Tracked in your browser
    only" → a live client shows "0 of 26 done"; state dies with the browser
    profile.
    **Direction:** persist server-side (it's single-admin, trivial), or at
    least label the risk prominently.

27. **Truth Vault leads with six empty pack sections.** Six "No X facts
    yet" shells dominate, while the 10 real facts are a footnote ("kept and
    still used for accuracy checks") with no way to view them.
    **Direction:** show existing facts first; collapse empty categories.

28. **Date format inconsistency, systemic.** "Checked 7/25/2026" (Authority)
    vs "4 Aug 2026" vs "Aug 4, 2026" vs "2026-08-06" (work log) — 34 ad-hoc
    `toLocaleDateString` calls across 27 files, several with no locale
    argument.
    **Direction:** one `formatDate()` util in `lib/`, lint against raw
    `toLocaleDateString`.

29. **Internal version tags visible in admin UI.** "stability_v1",
    "impact_v1", "Composite (v1.4.0)" render as UI text on the overview.
    Admin-only so allowed, but reads as debug output.
    **Direction:** tuck behind a tooltip/info icon.

30. **Test data pollution.** "ZZ Test Prospect DELETE / TWO DELETE" appear
    in the client list, gap matrix, and benchmarks (sorted first). Not an
    app bug, but it will photobomb any demo/screen-share.
    **Direction:** delete them; consider excluding prospects from analytics
    surfaces regardless.

31. **Stale-analysis nudges missing.** Content Roadmap "Generated Jul 9 ·
    from 12 lost queries" sits beside Content Gaps "analysed Aug 4"; a newer
    scan (4 Aug) exists but nothing suggests regenerating the roadmap.
    **Direction:** "newer scan available — regenerate" hint when generation
    predates the latest scan.

32. **Priority chips inconsistent casing.** "medium/high/low" lowercase on
    Delivery/Review Queue, "MEDIUM/LOW" uppercase on overview/client view.
    **Direction:** one Badge variant.

## Verified fine (no action needed)

- Mobile `/view/*` at 375px: no horizontal overflow; charts scale via
  viewBox; nav collapses to native `<select>` as specced.
- Language rules: no banned terms spotted on any client surface
  ("visibility frequency", "Seen by AI", "AI Search Ranking" all correctly
  used).
- Overview data fetching is properly parallelized (`Promise.all`, 9 calls) —
  slowness is per-endpoint latency + dev compile, not a request waterfall.
- Toolkit page and site-audit checklist are the cleanest, most consistent
  page in the app — good reference for the rest.

## If only three get fixed first

**#1/#3** (duplicate/contradictory evidence counts on the client overview),
**#6** (accuracy page contradicting itself), **#9** (no error/loading states
on `/view/*`). All three are client-visible and all three are trust-breaking
on the exact surface clients use to judge the product.
