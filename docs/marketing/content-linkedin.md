## Post 7 — What "AI Visibility" Actually Means (No Jargon)

Let me make this concrete.

Someone in KL opens Perplexity and types: "What's a good accounting firm in Kuala Lumpur?"

Perplexity pulls from everything it knows — articles, reviews, directories, content across the web — and gives a specific answer with named brands.

AI visibility is simply: does your brand get named in that answer?

Not "do you rank on page 1." Not "do you have good domain authority." Just: when the AI is asked a question in your space, does it know you exist and trust you enough to recommend you?

That's it. That's the whole concept. And most businesses have never measured it.

Think about how brand discovery used to work:

That dynamic still exists. But the "friend" is now often ChatGPT.

The brands that understand what those signals are, and how to strengthen them, are the ones that will own the AI word-of-mouth channel.

That's the new marketing frontier. And it's earlier than most people think.
